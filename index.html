Preface

The Project

Human beings spend most of their lives surrounded by artificial environments.

We wake beneath artificial light. We work inside artificial spaces. We move through cities disconnected from the rhythms of the Earth. We rarely stop to ask what we are actually connected to.

The Space Connection Project explores another possibility: what if we deliberately returned our attention to the sources of energy surrounding us?

  • The Sun
  • The Earth
  • The sky
  • The forest
  • Water
  • Silence
  • Other human beings
  • The atmosphere of a place

The project explores the idea that perhaps energy is not something we merely consume.

Perhaps it is something we can experience. Perhaps we can learn to notice it. Perhaps we can learn to receive it. Perhaps we can learn to transform our relationship with it.

We are learning to perceive

this world fully,

and connect to the space.

Obs. 01

The Sun

We have lived beneath the Sun for millions of years.

We built our calendars around it. Our cultures worshipped it. Our bodies evolved under it. Every day, without asking anything from us, it arrives.

Yet we rarely stop to experience it.

Solar Immersion

To eat the Sun is to become aware of what already enters every living system.

The light becomes warmth. The warmth becomes movement. The movement becomes life.

Enter the Field

Every place has an atmosphere.

Some places feel empty. Some feel alive. Some seem to hold memory.

Some change the way people speak. Some make us silent.

We are interested in those places.

We call them fields.

Obs. 06

The Unknown

We don't know exactly what energy is.

We don't claim to possess an answer.

We are interested in the experience of searching.

Perhaps energy is physical.

Perhaps it is psychological.

Perhaps it is relational.

Perhaps it is something we have not yet learned to describe.

We are curious enough to look.

Field Journal

A partial record of observations gathered across active sites. Locations withheld at the request of participating communities.

Field 001Logged
Sunrise

Observed changes in group behaviour during first light. Conversation slows. Several participants report warmth prior to physical contact with sunlight. No explanation offered.

Status — Recurring
Field 002Logged
Forest

Recorded prolonged silence among a group entering dense tree cover. Attention shifted from conversation to surroundings within minutes. Effect diminished near cleared paths.

Status — Recurring
Field 003Logged
Silence

Extended absence of sound produced measurable unease within the first ten minutes, followed by a marked shift toward calm. Participants described the silence as "louder" than expected.

Status — Under review
Field 004Unresolved
The Unknown

No consistent explanation found. The same pattern recurred at three unrelated sites, months apart. Filed as ongoing.

The title of Grande Cosmic Mentor is carried by the person entrusted with the integrity of the project's practices — the questions it asks, the language it uses, and the line it will not cross into certainty.
