The Sun

We have lived beneath the Sun for millions of years.

We built our calendars around it. Our cultures worshipped it. Our bodies evolved under it. Every day, without asking anything from us, it arrives.

Yet we rarely stop to experience it.

Solar Immersion

To eat the Sun is to become aware of what already enters every living system.

The light becomes warmth. The warmth becomes movement. The movement becomes life.
