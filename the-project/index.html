The Project

Human beings spend most of their lives surrounded by artificial environments.

We wake beneath artificial light. We work inside artificial spaces. We move through cities disconnected from the rhythms of the Earth. We rarely stop to ask what we are actually connected to.

The Space Connection Project explores another possibility: what if we deliberately returned our attention to the sources of energy surrounding us?

  • The Sun
  • The Earth
  • The sky
  • The forest
  • Water
  • Silence
  • Other human beings
  • The atmosphere of a place

The project explores the idea that perhaps energy is not something we merely consume.

Perhaps it is something we can experience. Perhaps we can learn to notice it. Perhaps we can learn to receive it. Perhaps we can learn to transform our relationship with it.
