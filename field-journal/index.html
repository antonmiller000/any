Field Journal

A partial record of observations gathered across active sites. Locations withheld at the request of participating communities.

Field 001Logged
Sunrise

Observed changes in group behaviour during first light. Conversation slows. Several participants report warmth prior to physical contact with sunlight. No explanation offered.

Status — Recurring
Field 002Logged
Forest

Recorded prolonged silence among a group entering dense tree cover. Attention shifted from conversation to surroundings within minutes. Effect diminished near cleared paths.

Status — Recurring
Field 003Logged
Silence

Extended absence of sound produced measurable unease within the first ten minutes, followed by a marked shift toward calm. Participants described the silence as "louder" than expected.

Status — Under review
Field 004Unresolved
The Unknown

No consistent explanation found. The same pattern recurred at three unrelated sites, months apart. Filed as ongoing.

Status — Open
