The Unknown

We don't know exactly what energy is.

We don't claim to possess an answer.

We are interested in the experience of searching.

Perhaps energy is physical.

Perhaps it is psychological.

Perhaps it is relational.

Perhaps it is something we have not yet learned to describe.

We are curious enough to look.
